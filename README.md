**Brainrot** — one word for the whole internet.

## Why this word wins

The internet is not a place or an app. It is the thing that happens to
your brain after you have been on it: an endless scroll of memes, drama,
hot takes and videos you cannot unwatch, until your thoughts themselves
start sounding like the feed. *Brainrot* names that in a single beat —
and it answers the judge's five criteria in order:

1. **One word.** Eight letters, no spaces, no hyphen, no list.
2. **Funny by itself.** It is a joke you get the instant you read it —
   the internet rotting your brain is the punchline, no setup needed.
3. **True of the whole internet.** Not one app, one site or one mood:
   every corner of the feed, from comments to videos to arguments,
   feeds the same rot.
4. **Surprising.** The obvious answers are chaos, cats, memes,
   addiction. *Brainrot* is the word the internet invented for itself.
5. **Lands in one beat.** Short, correctly spelled, instantly readable.

The best word makes a reader laugh and then nod. *Brainrot* does both:
you laugh, then you catch yourself scrolling, and you nod.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes `Brainrot` (8 characters, one word) to
`/output/entry.txt`, where the make.fun gate scorer reads it.
